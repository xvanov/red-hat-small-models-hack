# Gap analysis: hackathon system vs the concierge spec

Written Oct 18, 2026 against commit `a30e09e`. Compares what `tools/`, `operator/`, `gym/` and `console/` do today with `docs/spec/concierge.md`. Rule IDs (`R-...`) and checklist items (`C..`) refer to that spec.

Effort: **S** = under 2 hours, contained in one module plus a test. **M** = half a day, touches tools plus prompt plus Gym tasks. **L** = a day or more, needs new state, a clock or a new task family.

## What already matches the spec

These are solid and enforced by code, not just the prompt:

- **Style guards** in `send_message`: at most 2 sentences, at most one question, no long dashes (C1, C2, C4).
- **Language lock** for English and Spanish (C11).
- **Price grounding**: every dollar figure must be in the room or listing record (C6).
- **The 6-month floor**: never named before the renter asks for shorter; below 6 months refused (C26, C27; decision D23).
- **The strict gate**: `read_reply` (fine-tuned 0.6B reader) labels each item `answered | vague | no | exception | absent`; `upsert_renter` stores it; `propose_showing` refuses until all five items are exact (C19 calendar half, C20, C21).
- **Never claim a confirmed showing** before the calendar event is `confirmed` (C31).
- **Availability blocks** and overlap checks in `find_showing_slots` / `propose_showing` (C30).
- **Escalation with a draft** (`escalate_to_owner`), owner approve/deny in the console (`/api/approvals`).
- **Burst merge**: the router's `BATCH` decision answers several renter messages in one turn (C56).
- **Stop before submit**: live adapters never post or send without the owner (C55).
- **Never delete** a register row: no tool can (C58).
- **Measured**: held-out v2 shows these behaviors scoring well (state 95%, style 77%, escalated 94% for the final config; `docs/results.md`).

## Gaps, ordered by importance for a Messenger MVP demo

The demo story is one Marketplace inquiry carried from "is this available?" to a proposed showing, the owner's confirmation, and the post-tour application, in English and Spanish. Gaps that break that story come first.
