How it works

  1. 1

    Your message arrives

    From Messenger, SMS or email, into one inbox.

  2. 2

    Small models do the work

    Nemotron 3.5 Lightning (30B, 3B active) decides what to do and calls tools: listings, calendar, owner approval. A fine-tuned 0.6B reader turns your reply into exact fields (move-in date, lease length, phone). Plain code checks every draft before it goes out.

  3. 3

    You get a reply

    Anything outside its rules (price, exceptions) goes to the owner first.
